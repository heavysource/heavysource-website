Our Process

A Disciplined Procurement Workflow

Every request follows a clear process designed to improve communication, supplier accountability, schedule visibility, and execution.

01

Requirements Review

Review specifications, quantities, schedules, delivery requirements, and operational priorities.

02

Supplier Identification

Identify manufacturers, distributors, dealers, and specialty suppliers capable of supporting the request.

03

RFQ Management

Coordinate quotation requests, supplier questions, pricing, availability, and lead times.

04

Quote Evaluation

Compare pricing, technical compliance, commercial terms, availability, and delivery schedules.

05

Purchase Coordination

Coordinate supplier communication, documentation, order confirmation, and procurement follow-up.

06

Delivery Support

Monitor order progress and coordinate transportation through final delivery.
